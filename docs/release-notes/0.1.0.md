First public version of Steppe: play Cossacks 3 on an Apple silicon Mac with one click and your own Steam account.

**Needs:** Apple silicon, macOS 14.6+, about 16 GB free space, Cossacks 3 in your Steam library.

**Good to know**
- Steppe is open source and not notarized by Apple. On first launch use *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or the install script in the README).
- Performance was measured on one M3 Mac only; reports from other Macs are very welcome (see `docs/COMPATIBILITY.md`).
- Multiplayer is not verified. The optional geometry speed-up is off by default.

Verify your download with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
