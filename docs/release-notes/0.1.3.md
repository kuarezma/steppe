Fixes microphone permission for Steam and voice chat on MacBooks:

- Declares `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` (English and Turkish) and signs `Steppe.app` and Wine engine binaries with the `com.apple.security.device.audio-input` entitlement.
- Requests macOS microphone access before Steam initializes CoreAudio (`winecoreaudio.drv`) and automatically refreshes stale ad-hoc TCC records after app updates.
- Adds microphone permission status and reset/settings controls under *Settings → Game*.

Existing installs update automatically.
