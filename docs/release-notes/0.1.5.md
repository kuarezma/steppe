Hardens display mode planning, profile encoding, OpenGL pbuffer management, and download/filesystem recovery:

- **Ultrawide & multi-display mode snapping**: when a 21:9 or 32:9 monitor exposes no smaller modes of its own aspect ratio, lower presets now fall back cleanly to enumerable 16:9/16:10 modes within the preset pixel budget instead of staying stuck at full native resolution.
- **Lossless Delphi `profile.cfg` encoding**: preserves non-UTF-8 8-bit ANSI (`CP1251`/`CP1252`/`CP1254`) player names and handles reversed `Height`/`Width` key ordering without skipping resolution tuning.
- **Early-crash detection & screen hotplug**: detects exits under 15 seconds (including `00427A2B` access violations) to surface recovery hints on the Ready screen, and refreshes cached Windows display modes whenever monitors change, the laptop lid closes, or the Mac wakes from sleep.
- **OpenGL pbuffer slot reclamation (`glfix`)**: reference-counted slot reclamation prevents `MAXPB` (`4096`) exhaustion during long matches; `libglfix.dylib` is now explicitly signed inside the app bundle.
- **Resilient downloads & volume safety**: corrupted `.part` downloads are automatically purged and retried, unmounted `/Volumes` drives are detected before creating directories, and speed-library replacement rolls back safely if a file move fails.

Existing installs update automatically.
