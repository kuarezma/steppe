Automatic MacBook camera notch HUD layout for *Cossacks 3*:

- **Split top resource bar on notched MacBooks**: when playing on a MacBook with a camera notch (`safeAreaInsets.top > 0`), Steppe automatically splits the top resource bar around the notch — **Wood, Food, Stone** sit to the left of the notch and **Gold, Iron, Coal** sit to the right, so no resource counter is hidden behind the camera housing.
- **Zero impact on non-notched screens**: external monitors, non-notched Macs, and 16:10/16:9 display modes keep the original single-piece resource bar untouched; `.steppe-orig` backups are verified with SHA-256 and automatically restored when switching to an external display or running *Restore original game files*.

Existing installs update automatically.
